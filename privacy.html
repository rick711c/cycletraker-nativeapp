1. Overview

Flora is a menstrual cycle and pregnancy tracking application designed to run entirely offline on your mobile device. We built Flora with a privacy-first philosophy: your health data belongs to you and only you.

🔒 Zero Data Collection

Flora does not collect, transmit, store on any server, sell, share, or provide access to any personal or health data. We — the developers — have absolutely no ability to see or access your data at any time.

2. Data Storage

All data you enter into Flora is stored exclusively in a local SQLite database on your device hardware. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Period start and end dates
  • Cycle length and period length settings
  • Daily symptom, mood, and flow logs
  • User preferences (goal, notification settings)

No data is ever transmitted over the internet, to any cloud service, analytics platform, advertising network, or third party of any kind.

⚠️ Critical Data Warning

Because Flora stores all data locally and does not back up your data to any cloud server, uninstalling the app or clearing your device storage will result in the permanent and unrecoverable loss of all your tracked health data. We strongly recommend noting important dates separately if you plan to remove the app.

3. What We Do NOT Collect

  • No personal identifiers (name, email, phone number)
  • No device identifiers (IMEI, advertising ID)
  • No location data
  • No usage analytics or telemetry
  • No crash reports sent to external services
  • No cookies or tracking technologies

4. Third-Party Services

Flora does not integrate with any third-party data collection services. The app does not contain advertisements, analytics SDKs, or social media trackers.

5. Notifications

Flora may send local push notifications (e.g., period reminders) if you enable them. These notifications are generated and scheduled entirely on your device using the operating system's local notification APIs. No notification data is sent to any server.

6. Children's Privacy

Flora does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Since no data leaves the device, there is no risk of external data exposure regardless of user age.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the "Last updated" date at the top will change. Significant changes will be communicated through an in-app prompt requesting re-acknowledgment.
